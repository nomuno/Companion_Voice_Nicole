# Companion - Voice: Nicole

Voice pack for Companion (requires Companion 0.72+): Nicole's full voice (low, warm and calm). Adds her 1,052 zone comments and zone histories; her everyday lines are already built into Companion.

**Version 0.72** · Interface 16001 · WoW: Forever · by Darth

## Install with WowUp

1. In WowUp, pick your WoW: Forever installation.
2. Go to **Get Addons** → **Install from URL**.
3. Paste `https://github.com/nomuno/Companion_Voice_Nicole` and install. WowUp checks this repo's releases for updates.

## Manual install

Download `Companion_Voice_Nicole-0.72-forever.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/nomuno/Companion_Voice_Nicole/releases/latest) and unzip it into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns`.

## What's new in 0.72

- First release. Nicole's zone comments (790) and zone and landmark histories (262), moved out of the main Companion download.
- Unzip into Interface/AddOns next to Companion and restart WoW fully. Settings > AddOns > Companion > Audio shows it as Installed - full pack.
